# PaperHumanizer: academic revision resources

A small public resource collection from the PaperHumanizer product team for students, researchers, and writers using English as an additional language. These materials are free to read and copy without a product account.

## Worksheets and examples

- [Revision-receipt workbook and local exact-file check](revision-receipt-workbook.md): keep the source, candidate, and review decision separate; SHA-256 identifies bytes, not meaning. Added October 29, 2026.

- [Abstract word-budget worksheet and local counter](abstract-budget-worksheet.md): a fictional keep-list exercise and an offline whitespace counter with tests. Added October 28, 2026.

- [Response-to-reviewers worksheet and English language bank](reviewer-response-template.md): a change-log table, copyable response block, sentence frames, and a fictional worked example. Added October 23, 2026.
- [Academic rewrite review worksheet](revision-review-checklist.md): a worked synthetic example and document-export checks. Added October 21, 2026.
- [Local JavaScript fixture checker](revision-fixture-checker.mjs): a dependency-free starting point for checking lexical invariants. A lexical check cannot certify factual accuracy or source support.

Keep completed worksheets containing confidential reviews, unpublished manuscripts, or private results off public repositories and issues.

## About the product

I build [PaperHumanizer's academic editor](https://paperhumanizer.com/ai-humanizer?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=reviewer_response_20261023). It offers prose rewriting and original/revised text comparison. Generation requires sign-in and credits; consult the [live pricing page](https://paperhumanizer.com/pricing) for current access and offers.

Authors must check facts, numbers, citations, and claim strength against their sources and follow their institution's and journal's AI-use policies. We do not guarantee a detector result or publication acceptance.

These resources were prepared with AI assistance. They are product-team educational materials, not independent reviews or endorsements. Examples are synthetic, not measured product results.
