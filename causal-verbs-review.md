# Association or causation? A revision check for research writers

A free worksheet by the PaperHumanizer team, prepared with AI assistance. The examples are invented.

| Original | Candidate | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Students who attended more sessions scored higher. | More sessions improved scores. | Reject the causal upgrade. Attendance could correlate with other factors. |
| The intervention group scored higher at follow-up. | The intervention raised scores. | Check assignment, baseline, missing data and the reported analysis before accepting a causal verb. |
| The findings are consistent with better retention. | The findings confirm better retention. | Preserve uncertainty and check whether retention was measured directly. |

For each sentence record the comparison group, assignment method, outcome, time window, source locator and the strongest claim the source supports. If the study design or result is unclear, mark the rewrite unresolved. Keep these notes in an approved private workspace.

A language-only revision of the first example is “Higher attendance was associated with higher scores.” The association could still reflect selection or confounding. Ask the author before changing the interpretation.
